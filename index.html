About

A developer who learns by building.

Short version: I like making things that work, keeping them simple, and actually understanding how they work under the hood.

Hey — I'm Charlz. I go by that name online, and charlz.org is my home on the internet. I'm a Gorilla Tag Copy developer.

I've spent over a year developing games with the APK method — building Gorilla Tag–style games, working on the movement, the systems behind them, and the little details that make a copy actually play well. Most of that time went into iterating: testing how it feels, tweaking, and going back again until it's right.

I'm self-taught, which means everything I know came from building real things and fixing real problems rather than following a script.

This page is a plain profile — who I am, what I build, and what I've spent my time on.
